Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Officethree Technologies Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website or use our products.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, email address, and message content submitted through our contact form
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, browser type, device information, and referral source
  • Cookies and local storage — theme preferences and basic analytics identifiers

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • Improve our website and products based on usage patterns
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of our services
  • Communicate about our products and services when requested

Cookies and Local Storage

Our website uses minimal cookies and local storage. We store your theme preference (light/dark mode) in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Third-Party Services

Our products and website may use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — backend infrastructure, authentication, and database services
  • Razorpay — payment processing for our restaurant platform
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery
  • Web3Forms — contact form processing

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our products use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and follow security best practices.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain our business records. Usage data is retained in aggregated, anonymized form.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of any future communications from us
  • Clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at hello@officethree.com.
